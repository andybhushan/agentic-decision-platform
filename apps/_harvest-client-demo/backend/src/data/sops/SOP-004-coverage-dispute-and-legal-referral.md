# SOP-004 — Coverage Dispute & Legal Referral

## 1. Purpose
Coverage decisions determine whether and to what extent a loss is payable.
Getting them right — and defensible — protects policyholders and the business.
This SOP sets when a coverage question must be referred to Legal / Coverage
Counsel before a position is taken.

## 2. Direct Referral Principle
Coverage / Legal referral is **direct** and available to any adjuster regardless
of seniority. It does not require Claims Supervisor sign-off. Authority
escalation (SOP-002) is separate and only applies if the exposure exceeds the
adjuster's limit.

## 3. When to Refer to Legal
Refer before issuing a coverage position when any of the following apply:
- The policy language is ambiguous or reasonably open to more than one reading.
- An exclusion may apply but its application to the facts is unclear.
- There is a dispute about coverage scope, limits, deductibles, or conditions.
- Late notice, non-disclosure, or breach-of-condition arguments are in play.
- Litigation has been served or credibly threatened.
- A denial or reservation-of-rights position is being considered on contested facts.

## 4. Handling Rules
1. Do **not** communicate a denial, restriction, or reservation of rights to the
   claimant on an ambiguous or disputed coverage point before Legal review.
2. Map the specific policy clause(s) to the specific incident facts on the file so
   Legal has a clear question to answer.
3. Distinguish clearly between what the policy states, what is factually
   established, and what remains ambiguous.
4. Where coverage is clear, proceed under normal handling — routine, unambiguous
   coverage confirmations do not need Legal referral.

## 5. Guidance for the Digital Steward
- When coverage ambiguity or dispute is present, recommend a **direct Legal /
  Coverage Counsel referral** and flag which clause and which facts create the
  ambiguity.
- Never state that a coverage position may be issued to the claimant before Legal
  review when the point is genuinely disputed or ambiguous.
- Never fabricate policy language: if the clause text is not in context, say so
  rather than inferring it.
- Only mention Claims Supervisor where exposure exceeds the adjuster's authority
  limit (SOP-002), as a separate action.

## 6. Audit & Records
The coverage question, the clause/fact mapping, the referral, and Legal's response
are recorded on the claim file and are auditable by compliance.
