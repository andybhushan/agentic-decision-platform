# SOP-003 — SIU / Fraud Referral Procedure

## 1. Purpose
The Special Investigations Unit (SIU) investigates suspected claim fraud. Early,
direct referral protects the business and honest policyholders while preserving
fair treatment of the claimant, who remains presumed legitimate until findings
are established.

## 2. Direct Referral Principle
Any adjuster who identifies credible fraud indicators refers the claim to SIU
**directly and immediately**. Fraud referral is:
- **Not gated by seniority** — a Junior adjuster may refer just as a Lead can.
- **Not gated by Claims Supervisor sign-off** — the referral does not "route up"
  through a supervisor first.
- **Independent of authority limits** — SIU referral is about investigation, not
  settlement authority. Authority escalation (SOP-002) only applies separately if
  the exposure also exceeds the adjuster's limit.

## 3. Fraud Indicators (non-exhaustive)
- Staged-loss or damage geometry inconsistent with the described incident.
- Inconsistent, evolving, or coached claimant statements.
- Delayed notification without credible explanation.
- Prior-claim linkage (shared phone numbers, addresses, vehicles, witnesses).
- Pressure for rapid cash settlement; reluctance to provide documentation.
- Evidence tampering, altered documents, or backdated paperwork.

A single strong indicator, or a cluster of weaker ones, is sufficient to refer.
Adjusters are not required to prove fraud before referring — SIU makes that
assessment.

## 4. Referral Steps
1. Record the specific indicator(s) and supporting evidence on the claim file.
2. Raise the SIU referral within **1 business day** of identifying the indicator.
3. Place any settlement on hold and maintain the reserve while under review — do
   not progress to settlement, denial, or any coverage position tied to the
   suspected fraud until SIU responds or gives interim direction.
4. Do not disclose the SIU referral or fraud suspicion to the claimant or third
   parties.
5. Continue non-prejudicial handling steps (e.g. coverage verification,
   documentation gathering) in parallel where they do not interfere with the
   investigation.

## 5. While Under SIU Review
- Keep the claim in SIU review status; keep the settlement block and reserve hold.
- Request targeted SIU updates on the specific open points (e.g. damage analysis,
  linkage checks, notification timeline).
- Document verified facts, disputed findings, and open questions distinctly so the
  file stays decision-ready when SIU responds.

## 6. Guidance for the Digital Steward
- When fraud indicators are present, recommend a **direct SIU referral** as the
  primary escalation and never present Claims Supervisor approval as a
  precondition for it.
- Only mention Claims Supervisor if the exposure independently exceeds the
  adjuster's authority limit (SOP-002), and then only as a separate, parallel
  action.
- Reinforce the settlement hold and reserve maintenance while SIU is engaged.

## 7. Audit & Records
The referral, indicators, timestamp, and SIU correspondence are logged on the
claim file and are auditable by compliance.
