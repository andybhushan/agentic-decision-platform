# SOP-015 — Claimant Communication & Notifications

## 1. Purpose
This procedure standardises claimant communication so the claimant is kept informed
promptly and transparently, through their preferred channel, at every significant
point in their claim — consistent with fair-claims-handling principles.

## 2. Preferred Channel
Each claimant has a preferred contact channel — **email**, **SMS**, or **phone** —
recorded on their persona and on the claim. All outbound communication uses the
preferred channel unless the claimant requests otherwise or the message type
requires a specific channel (e.g. a signed document).

## 3. Notification Triggers
Communications are sent at defined lifecycle events:

**Automatic notifications:**
- Claim submitted successfully.
- Evidence / more-information request (see SOP-007).
- Coverage decision (covered / excluded / ambiguous — see SOP-004).
- Settlement offer.
- Payment issued.
- Claim closed.

**Manual (adjuster-initiated) notifications:**
- A custom message or claim-note update, sent via the claimant's preferred channel.

## 4. Content Standards
Every claimant communication must be clear, accurate, and specific:

- An **evidence request** states exactly what is needed and by when.
- A **coverage decision** states the position in plain language; an adverse or
  ambiguous position is only communicated **after** the required human review /
  Legal referral (SOP-004, SOP-010) — never pre-emptively.
- A **settlement offer** states the amount and basis.
- No coverage position or denial is communicated to a claimant before the governing
  decision has cleared its required approval gate.

## 5. Service-Tier Handling
Communication style and ownership follow the client service tier (SOP-009):
signature / white-glove clients receive human-managed communication (and may be
represented by an appointed representative), while standard-tier communications may
be automated within governance. Regardless of tier, the content standards in §4
apply.

## 6. Timeliness
Notifications are issued promptly at the triggering event. Evidence requests are
sent as soon as the gap is identified so the claimant has a fair opportunity to
respond within the claim's SLA (SOP-009).

## 7. Digital Steward Guidance
The Digital Steward may draft or recommend claimant communications and identify
when a notification is due, but the decision content it references must have cleared
its governance gate first. It does not itself send a coverage decision or denial.
It remains advisory (see SOP-013).

## 8. Audit & Records
Every claimant communication — trigger, channel, content summary, and timestamp —
is recorded on the claim's audit trail (SOP-012).
